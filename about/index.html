ABOUT ME

Developer by conviction.
Creator by enthusiasm.

I’m Alexander Dennhoven, known in the developer community as alexgamingdev. I combine hands-on software development with a passion for making knowledge easy to understand.

Curiosity leads to better solutions

I’m interested in the questions behind a technical challenge: What problem are we solving? What do the people using the solution need? And how can we build software that is clear, dependable and maintainable over time?

I value clear concepts, clean code and software architecture that serves its purpose instead of adding needless complexity. Good software comes from careful problem-solving, continuous learning and exchanging different perspectives.

Making technology easier to understand

As a creator, I publish technical content as alexgamingdev. On YouTube, I share insights into software development and explain complex topics clearly. GitHub is where you can explore my projects and open-source contributions.

Building and explaining go hand in hand: teaching makes ideas clearer, and exchanging perspectives brings new ones into my work.
